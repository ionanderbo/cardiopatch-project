Section 3.3.1 · Participant Selection (Table 6)

Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria

Inclusion Criteria
  • Adults ≥18 years old (prioritizing high-risk adults >65 years).
  • Prior cardiovascular history, hypertension, or type 2 diabetes.
  • Residents in rural areas with limited access to specialized cardiology.
  • Ability to provide written informed consent and wear a chest patch.
Exclusion Criteria
  • Severe skin allergies, contact dermatitis, or active chest lesions.
  • Implanted active cardiac devices (pacemakers, ICDs) causing signal interference.
  • Critically ill patients requiring immediate intensive care unit admission.
Section 3.3.2 · Validation Assessment Tests (Table 7)

Three-Phase Pilot Study Protocol

Phase 1 · BIOTECH Lab

Signal Fidelity Benchmark

What & How: Simultaneous recording comparing CardioPatch ECG & temperature against a calibrated reference clinical ECG monitor (SNR, R-peak sensitivity).

Phase 2 · Home / Living Lab

24–72h Daily Activity Trial

What & How: Continuous wear during walking, sleep, and showering to validate IP67 adhesion, BLE packet delivery, and false-alarm rate.

Phase 3 · Post-Trial

SUS & Clinician Triage Audit

What & How: System Usability Scale (SUS target >80) with older patients/caregivers + semi-structured triage review with cardiologists.
