SECTION 2.3 · QUALITATIVE FIELD RESEARCH

Shaped by Cardiologists, Nurses, Patients & Caregivers.

Four semi-structured interviews conducted between 20/10/2026 and 23/10/2026 directly determined our Edge AI filtering, IP67 silicone hardware, and calm dual-tier interface.

INTERVIEW 01 · 20/10/2026 · IN PERSON

Dr. Jon Azpeitia

Specialist in Cardiology

Alarm Fatigue
“If your AI patch sends me 15 notifications a day for simple sinus tachycardia while a patient walks up stairs, doctors will throw the system away within a week.”

Engineering Translation: On-patch TinyML classifies benign exertion silently for daily summaries and transmits only a focused 30-second pre- and post-event ECG strip when ischemic/arrhythmic thresholds are crossed.

INTERVIEW 02 · 21/10/2026 · IN PERSON

Mikel Unanue

Lead Nurse, Cardiac Rehabilitation

IP67 & Skin
“Patients stop wearing patches for two main reasons: skin itchiness from cheap hydrogels and having to take it off every time they shower.”

Engineering Translation: Modular architecture pairing a reusable electronics core with disposable, breathable medical-grade hypoallergenic silicone adhesives rated IP67 water-resistant for uninterrupted showering.

INTERVIEW 03 · 21/10/2026 · ONLINE

Javier Mendizabal

Patient, 68 yrs · Hypertension & Prior AMI

Calm Patient UI
“After my heart attack, every minor twinge makes me freeze. If the patch starts beeping or flashing red by mistake, my blood pressure will spike from fear.”

Engineering Translation: Zero red panic lights on the patient app—displaying a serene, neutral confirmation (“System Active · Heart Rhythm Stable”) tailored for older adults.

INTERVIEW 04 · 23/10/2026 · ONLINE

Carmen Mendizabal

Informal Caregiver / Daughter

Escalation Tree
“I live away from my father. I don't need to check his heart rate every minute, but I want the app to ring loud if something actually goes wrong.”

Engineering Translation: Automated 3-tier escalation ladder: gentle patient check-in first, loud caregiver push alert second, and clinician/emergency triage dispatch if unacknowledged.

SECTION 3.3.1 · TABLE 6

Participant Criteria

✓ Inclusion Criteria

  • Adults ≥18 years old (prioritizing ≥65 senior cohort).
  • Prior cardiac history, hypertension, or diabetes risk factors.
  • Residents in rural areas with limited cardiology access.
  • Ability to provide signed informed consent & wear chest patch.

✕ Exclusion Criteria

  • Severe dermatological allergies or active chest skin lesions.
  • Implanted electronic cardiac devices (pacemakers / ICDs).
  • Critically ill patients requiring immediate inpatient ICU care.
SECTION 3.3.2 · TABLE 7

3-Phase Validation & Assessment Tests

Phase 1: Laboratory ECG Benchmark BIOTECH Lab · Once

Compare patch R-peak detection, ST-segment fidelity, and NTC thermal accuracy against clinical reference ECG equipment & MIT-BIH/PTB-XL waveforms.

Phase 2: 24–72h Ambulatory Home Pilot Living Lab / Home · 72h

Continuous daily living monitoring (walking, sleeping, showering with IP67 patch) evaluating adhesive retention, false-alarm suppression, and BLE/Cloud sync.

Phase 3: System Usability Scale (SUS) & Interviews Post-Pilot · Patients & MDs

Standardized 10-item SUS questionnaire (target score >80/100) + post-trial interviews with high-risk patients, caregivers, and cardiologists.

SECTION 4.2 · TABLE 8

Project & Clinical Risk Analysis Matrix

RISK 01 · CLINICAL Prob: Med · Impact: High

Clinician Alarm Fatigue

False-positive alerts during benign physical exertion (stairs, brisk walking) cause doctors to ignore notifications.

Mitigation: Dual-tier Edge TinyML filtering + multi-parameter ECG ST-depression & skin thermal drop confirmation.

RISK 02 · ERGONOMIC Prob: Med · Impact: High

Sweat Detachment & Dermatitis

Hydrogel irritation or patch detachment during showering interrupts continuous 24/7 monitoring.

Mitigation: Medical-grade breathable hypoallergenic silicone adhesive + IP67 water-resistant modular snap housing.

RISK 03 · CONNECTIVITY Prob: Low · Impact: Med

Rural Cellular Dead Zones

Intermittent internet in rural areas delays cloud upload of telemetry logs.

Mitigation: On-patch Edge AI runs 100% offline with 72h local flash memory & automatic store-and-forward sync.
