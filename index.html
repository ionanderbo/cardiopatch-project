Chapter 1.2 · Selected Topic Justification

A wearable system purpose-built to catch unfelt ischemia.

Cardiovascular diseases claim 17.9 million lives annually (32% of all global deaths) [12], yet roughly 45% of myocardial infarctions occur silently without classic chest pain [14]. Traditional 12-lead ECGs and short-term Holter monitors capture only narrow snapshots—leaving high-risk seniors, diabetic patients, and rural residents unprotected.

Disclaimer: CardioPatch 4.0 is an early-warning clinical decision-support tool; it does not replace physicians or guarantee prediction of every acute cardiac event.
