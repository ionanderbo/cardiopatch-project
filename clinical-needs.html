The Gap In Standard Care

Short hospital tests miss sudden warning signs.

Limited snapshot tests: Standard clinic ECGs and short-term monitors only record heart activity during brief windows, letting sudden intermittent changes pass unnoticed.

Vulnerable & rural communities: Older adults, people living with hypertension or diabetes, and residents in rural areas with limited access to specialized cardiology face the highest risk of delayed diagnosis.
