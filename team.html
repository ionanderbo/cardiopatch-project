Section 5.1 · Technical Conclusions

Bridging the Outpatient Diagnostic Gap

CardioPatch 4.0 integrates continuous single-lead ECG and skin thermometry inside an IP67 breathable chest patch with two-tier Edge AI and a decoupled 3-tier Django/Docker cloud platform. By filtering benign exertion locally and transmitting 30-second pre/post-event strips, the system addresses the 45% silent myocardial infarction gap while preventing clinical alarm fatigue.

Limitations & Future Work: The current prototype serves strictly as an early-warning clinical decision-support aid; clinical multi-center trials and CE Class IIa medical device certification remain essential next steps.

Section 5.2 · Personal Conclusions

Co-Designing Around Human Behavior

Developing this Health 4.0 solution taught Group 06 that hardware sensitivity alone cannot save lives if clinicians disable noisy alerts or anxious patients remove uncomfortable sensors. Semi-structured interviews with Dr. Jon Azpeitia, Mikel Unanue, Javier Mendizabal, and Carmen Mendizabal reshaped every layer of our engineering—from IP67 silicone adhesion to the calm D4ALL patient interface and 3-step caregiver escalation tree.
